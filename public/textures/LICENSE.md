# Bundled textures

Both textures come from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/) and are published
under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public
domain — attribution not required, given anyway).

| File | Source asset | Processing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `paper.jpg` | [Paper005](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper005) | color map → grayscale, autocontrast, 512² |
| `wood.jpg` | [Wood051](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Wood051) | color map → grayscale, autocontrast, 512² |

They are tiled at low alpha over the game's vector fills (UI lacquer panels,
speech-bubble paper, the counter slab) purely as surface grain; all colour
still comes from the palette.
